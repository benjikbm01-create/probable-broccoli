# THE FORGOTTEN ISLAND — Story Bible (⚠ full spoilers)

## Premise
Ysmere is a small island whose ancient builders, the **Lanternwrights**, raised a
great lantern burning the **Ember** — a stone of living light that *writes the
island into the world's memory*. While it burns, the world remembers Ysmere.
When it is dark, a grey **Tide** (the fog) rises and the island is slowly
forgotten: ships pass it by, letters never arrive, maps go blank — and the
islanders' own memories fray, starting with whatever stood nearest the flame.

## What happened (thirty-one years ago)
- **Edric Vael**, Warden of Kestrel Keep, kept the lantern. His wife **Annis**
  and their six-year-old daughter **Elspeth ("Elsie")** were swept off the
  harbour wall in a great storm.
- Tobias Wake took his boat, the *Meridian*, out searching. The *Meridian*
  sank in the bay; Tobias was pulled ashore. He has forgotten why he sailed.
- At dawn Edric, believing both lost, read the Lanternwright murals: *"What the
  sea takes, the Wellspring keeps."* He took the Ember from the lantern and
  carried it down into the Undercroft to call his family back from the
  Wellspring, against the murals' warning: *"Never carry the flame below."*
- The lantern went dark. The Tide rose. Edric never came back up; grief and
  the Wellspring's memories bound him there. He became the **Drowned Warden**.
- That same dawn **Maud Pike** (Wendel's late wife) found little Elspeth alive
  on the shingle, wearing a silver gull locket, too shocked to say her name.
  Maud brought her to the Moss family at the inn. The Tide took the girl's
  memories of her parents first (they were nearest the flame). The Mosses
  raised her as **Hester Moss**.
- Over the years the islanders forgot the Warden's name. Graves and records
  of the Keep faded. Families who tried to row out were turned back by the fog.

## The letter
In moments of clarity Edric wrote letters and gave them to the Tide. One washed
up on a mainland shore and reached the player:
*"Ysmere is fading from the world, and it is my fault... Find the light that went
out and carry it home — even if I beg you not to... — E.V."*
The initials point two ways: Edric Vael (the writer) and Elspeth Vael (Hester).

## Twists (revealed in this order)
1. (M3, Keep study) The Warden took the Ember below for his lost family.
2. (M4, Hall of Echoes, echo 3) A little girl with a gull locket survived — Maud
   found her and brought her to the inn → Hester is the Warden's daughter.
   Optional earlier clues: Hester's lullaby ("I came to Mother Moss already
   humming it"), the inn cellar ledger, Maud's diary, the child's drawing.
3. (M4, vault lectern) The letter was written by the Warden himself.
4. (M5) The boss is Edric. Defeated, his echo can be told his daughter lived.
5. (M7) With the lantern relit, memories return: Hester remembers being Elspeth.

## Main objectives
1. **A Letter from Nowhere** — arrive; Tobias → Hester → Ione identifies the
   kestrel seal of the Wardens of Kestrel Keep.
2. **The Old Road** — the road to the Keep is sealed by a thorn wall; the
   Singing Stones open it with the old lullaby (Hester knows it; Wren knows the
   way). Puzzle 1: strike the stones in the song's order (heron, stag, owl, hare).
3. **The Silent Keep** — explore; read the Warden's log; Puzzle 2 (Sun Court
   mirrors) opens the Lantern Tower; the lantern is empty; the Warden's Key.
4. **Beneath the Keep** — unlock the cistern; Hall of Echoes (three memory
   pools); Puzzle 3 (gliding rune stones) opens the vault.
5. **The Wellspring** — the Drowned Warden (boss). His last words; the Ember.
6. **The Last Light** — climb the tower; relight the lantern (beacon beam).
7. **Homecoming** — the festival; Hester's revelation; credits; free play.

## Side quests
- **Cold Forge** (Corin): bring Kestrel steel from the Keep armoury → Kestrel Blade.
- **The Captain's Log** (Tobias): Hester's Diver's Draught; the sunken *Meridian*
  in the bay → logbook (Tobias remembers searching for the Warden's family) →
  Harbourmaster's Spyglass.
- **The Waystone Lamps** (Wendel): light four waystones → Wendel's Charm (totem)
  and Maud's diary (a clue about the girl on the shore).

### Wren Ashdown — ranger of the Mosswood (27)
Blunt, dry, restless, fiercely loyal. Her mother Rowan (the last ranger) died
five years ago; the Tide is erasing her mother's face from Wren's memory.
Quest **Her Mother's Bow**: Rowan's bow lies in the Hollow Oak, now a spider
nest; Wren can't make herself go back in. Date: **the firefly glade** at night.
Gift she treasures: the **Owl Whistle** (belltower secret). Best friend: Sol.

### Ione Calder — archivist (31)
Precise, anxious, wry, unexpectedly brave. Rereads her "Book of Days" every
morning so the Tide can't take her life from her. Quest **The Missing Pages**:
three chronicle pages (Wendel; the Keep study; Pell's store). The third is in
her own handwriting, written years ago and forgotten. Date: **stargazing on the
Archive roof**. Gift: the **Sea-Lily** (Gull Rock). Takes tea with Hester daily.

### Marisol "Sol" Reyes — boatwright (29)
Bold, teasing, warm, impulsive; aches to sail past the fog and fears there's
nothing out there. Quest **The Gull's Wing**: brass compass (wreck), sailcloth
(Keep barracks), heartwood spar (fallen pine, cut by Wren). Date: **sunset row to
Gull Rock**. Gift: the **Old Star Chart** (cliff cave). Mentor: Tobias.

Lantern relit → fog lifts → evening festival in the square → each friend or
partner has an epilogue scene → Hester remembers she is Elspeth Vael → the pier
→ credits (personalised) → free play with post-game dialogue.
