# The Forgotten Island — Player Guide (spoiler-free)

*A short, handcrafted adventure for Minecraft Java 1.21.1. Expect roughly 2–3 hours, more if you go hunting for every secret and get to know everyone.*

A letter washed up on a mainland shore, asking you to come to an island no chart remembers. You row into the fog. When it thins, there are lights ahead...

This guide tells you how to play, not what happens.

---

## Controls
| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move, look, jump, fight | your normal Minecraft controls |
| Talk to someone | **Right-click** them, then click an answer (or press **1–9**) |
| Skip the text animation | click, **Space** or **Enter** |
| Open your journal | **J**, or right-click the journal item |
| Get a hint | **H**, or "Reveal a hint" in the journal |
| Hide/show the objective tracker | **O** |
| Interact with the world | **Right-click** doors, levers, buttons, chests, lecterns and some special objects |

You can rebind the J/H/O keys under *Options → Controls → Key Binds → The Forgotten Island*.

## The basics
- **Adventure mode.** You can't break or place blocks; the island is handcrafted and protected. Everything you need can be reached on foot (and occasionally by swimming or climbing ladders and vines).
- **Your journal** tracks the main story, side quests, personal stories, the people you've met, the secrets you've found, and hints.
- **The objective tracker** (top-left) always shows the next step of the main story.
- **Hints** come in three steps for each objective, from a gentle nudge to the answer. Use them as much or as little as you like. The old man on the western shore is also happy to give advice.
- **Checkpoints.** If you fall in battle you return at the last checkpoint with **all your items**, and any fight you were in resets, so you can try again.
- **Saving** is automatic, like any Minecraft world. You can quit at any time.
- **Food.** The innkeeper will always feed you, and you can rest at the inn to pass the time of day.

## Getting around
- The island has four main regions: a harbour village, a dense forest, a coastal fortress and something beneath it.
- Paths connect everything. Some areas are closed at first and open as the story moves on.
- Shortcuts open as you progress, so you'll rarely have to walk the long way twice.
- Trails are marked with signposts. Look out for old **waystone lamps** along them.

## People
Gullhaven is small, and everyone in it has a life. People keep their own daily routines (work, meals, visiting friends, sleeping at night), so where you find someone depends on the time of day.
- If you can't find someone, try a different time of day, or check the journal's **People** page.
- Some conversations only happen in certain places or at certain times. You'll always be told where and when.
- Talk to people more than once. What they say changes as the story moves on and as you get to know them.

### Friendship and romance
Three of the island's residents have personal stories you can become part of. You can stay friends, or let something more grow, with **one, two, all three, or none** of them. Relationships grow through your choices in conversation and through helping people, not by spamming gifts. The main story can always be finished without any romance.

## Fighting
Combat is ordinary Minecraft combat with vanilla weapons and armour. You'll find better gear as you explore, and a few residents may help you out. Bring food, use your shield, and don't be afraid to retreat.

## Puzzles
There are three main puzzles. Each one uses ordinary right-click interaction, and each has a reset (or simply resets if you get it wrong), so you can never lock yourself out. The journal's hints escalate if you get stuck.

## Secrets
There are **12 secrets** hidden around the island: high places, low places, behind and beneath things. Each one rewards you with an item, a story fragment, or both. The journal counts how many you've found.

## When the story ends
After the credits you're free to keep exploring, finish side stories, find the remaining secrets and visit the people you've grown close to.

## Accessibility notes
- Dialogue appears at your own pace; nothing is timed.
- Story text never appears only in chat. It's always in a dialogue box, a book or the journal.
- Turn the objective tracker off with **O** if you prefer a clean screen.
