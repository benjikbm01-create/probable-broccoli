# The Forgotten Island

*A complete, handcrafted single-player adventure for **Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1** (Fabric).*

A letter washed up on a mainland shore asks you to come to an island that no chart remembers. Row into the fog, find the harbour village of Gullhaven, and uncover what happened to the Isle of Ysmere.

- One compact island (about 400 × 400 blocks): a harbour village, a dense forest, a coastal fortress, and what lies beneath.
- An original mystery with a beginning, middle and end; seven main objectives, three side quests, three puzzles, a final encounter, and 12 secrets.
- Seven named residents with their own daily routines and custom-skinned humanoid models. No villagers.
- Optional friendships and romances: one, two, all three, or none.
- A journal, an objective tracker, three-step hints, checkpoints, and ending credits.

## Play
See **[docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md)**. In short: install Fabric for 1.21.1, copy `dist/mods/*.jar` into `mods/`, unzip `dist/TheForgottenIsland-world.zip` into `saves/`, and play.

Then read the spoiler-free **[Player Guide](docs/PLAYER_GUIDE.md)**.
